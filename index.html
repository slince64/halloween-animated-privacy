Scope This Privacy Policy applies to Christmas Animated Watch Face on Garmin Connect IQ. “We” and “us” refer to the developer identified on the watch face’s store listing. This policy describes data handling through the watch face and our support channels. Garmin platforms and third-party payment services process information under their own privacy policies. 2. Information accessed by the watch face • Time, date and battery level: used to display the clock, seconds, date and remaining device battery. • Steps and a recent heart-rate value: read through Garmin device interfaces for display on the watch face. • Display preferences: theme, animation mode and 12/24-hour settings are saved through Garmin’s application settings mechanism. Steps and heart-rate values are processed on the watch. The watch face does not send these health values to the developer or KPAY, or create a separate health-history database. It does not access GPS location, contacts, photos or microphone content, and contains no advertising trackers or user-behaviour analytics tools. 3. Four-hour trial, payments and licensing The watch face offers a 4-hour free trial and uses KiezelPay (KPAY) to manage trial status and payment licensing. To verify entitlement, it sends KPAY the product identifier, device model/part number, a device or installation identification token, platform information and technical information needed for licence requests. The token may be based on a device identifier provided by Garmin or generated by the SDK; it identifies the licence recipient. The watch face stores the trial start time, licence status, identification token, payment code and necessary verification records locally. These records support trial management, payment prompts and unlock verification. They do not include transmitted health values. Email, payment or billing information you enter on checkout websites is processed by KPAY and its payment providers under the notices shown during checkout. The watch face itself does not receive or store full payment-card numbers or card security codes. The developer may access transaction or licensing information through the licensing dashboard or support channels as needed to resolve purchase issues. 4. Support information and sharing If you voluntarily contact us through “Contact Developer” or another support channel, we may receive the contact details, messages, watch model, screenshots and payment/licence references you provide. We use these to respond, troubleshoot and handle related requests. Please avoid sending full card numbers, passwords or unnecessary health records. We do not sell or rent personal information or provide health values accessed by the watch face to advertisers. Beyond the licensing and support purposes described above, information is disclosed only as necessary to comply with applicable law or lawful requests from authorities. 5. Retention and security The developer does not separately retain steps or heart-rate values as health-history records. Settings and licence information may be stored on the device to operate the watch face. Local data changes resulting from removal or reset depend on Garmin’s device and platform mechanisms. Removing the watch face does not automatically delete third-party transaction records. Support information is retained only as needed to resolve requests, provide necessary follow-up support or satisfy legal retention requirements, then deleted or de-identified. KPAY, payment providers and Garmin set their own retention periods. Licence verification uses HTTPS. We take safeguards proportionate to the information we handle, but cannot guarantee that any device or network transmission is entirely risk-free. 6. Your choices and data rights You can change watch-face settings, manage permissions where Garmin provides this option, or remove the watch face to stop its access. Removal does not delete Garmin’s original health or activity records; manage those through Garmin’s device and account features. To request access, correction or deletion of personal information you provided to the developer, or to exercise applicable rights to restrict or object to processing or other data rights, use “Contact Developer” on the watch face’s store listing. We may request necessary identity or transaction verification to protect your information. Requests concerning information held by KPAY or payment providers should be directed to those providers; we can offer reasonable assistance. 7.
