隱私權政策

本隱私權政策適用於 Garmin Connect IQ 平台上的 Halloween Animated Watch Face（以下簡稱「本錶面」）。 我們重視使用者隱私，並盡可能讓錶面在裝置本機完成資料處理。

1. 本錶面使用的資料

為了顯示錶面資訊，本錶面可能讀取 Garmin 裝置或 Garmin 系統提供的資料，例如：

  • 時間與日期
  • 心率
  • 步數與活動資料
  • 電池電量
  • 溫度與天氣狀態
  • 使用者於錶面設定中的主題與動畫偏好

這些資料主要用於即時顯示錶面內容，並在 Garmin 裝置或 Garmin 應用程式環境內處理。 開發者不會建立自己的伺服器來集中儲存上述健康、活動或裝置資料。

2. 天氣資料

天氣資訊由 Garmin 平台或 Garmin 可用的天氣服務提供。 本錶面只使用 Garmin 提供的天氣狀態與溫度資訊來顯示對應圖示與數值。

3. KPay 授權與購買驗證

本錶面使用 KPay 進行購買、試用或授權驗證。 驗證過程可能需要網路連線，並由 KPay 依其服務條款與隱私權政策處理必要的授權或交易識別資訊。 本錶面開發者不會直接接收或儲存您的信用卡或付款卡資料。

4. 設定資料

主題、動畫模式及其他錶面偏好可能儲存在 Garmin 裝置、Garmin Connect / Connect IQ 應用程式， 或 Garmin 所提供的應用程式設定儲存空間中，以便下次使用時保留設定。

5. 個人資料分享與販售

開發者不會販售您的個人資料，也不會為廣告目的將您的健康、活動或裝置資料分享給第三方。

6. 兒童隱私

本錶面不是專門針對兒童設計的服務，也不會主動要求使用者提供姓名、電子郵件、地址或其他可直接識別身分的個人資訊。

7. 資料安全

本錶面盡量避免將錶面資料傳送至開發者控制的外部伺服器。 Garmin 與 KPay 服務本身的資料安全措施，則分別受其各自的平台政策約束。

8. 隱私權政策更新

若未來新增需要額外資料存取的功能，本政策可能更新。 最新版本將公布於本頁面。

9. 聯絡方式

如對本隱私權政策或 Halloween Animated Watch Face 有任何問題， 請透過 Garmin Connect IQ Store 中的開發者聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Halloween Animated Watch Face on the Garmin Connect IQ platform (“the Watch Face”). We respect user privacy and aim to process watch-face data locally on the device whenever possible.

1. Data Used by the Watch Face

The Watch Face may access data made available by the Garmin device or Garmin platform, including:

  • Time and date
  • Heart rate
  • Steps and activity-related data
  • Battery level
  • Temperature and weather conditions
  • Theme and animation preferences selected in watch-face settings

This information is primarily used to display watch-face content in real time and is processed within the Garmin device or Garmin application environment. The developer does not operate a separate server for collecting or storing your health, activity, or device data.

2. Weather Data

Weather information is provided through Garmin or weather services available through the Garmin platform. The Watch Face uses weather conditions and temperature data only to display the appropriate icon and value.

3. KPay Licensing and Purchase Verification

The Watch Face uses KPay for purchase, trial, and license verification. Verification may require an internet connection, and KPay may process the identifiers necessary to provide licensing or transaction verification under its own terms and privacy policy. The Watch Face developer does not directly receive or store your credit-card or payment-card information.

4. Settings Data

Theme selection, animation mode, and other watch-face preferences may be stored on the Garmin device, in Garmin Connect / Connect IQ, or in Garmin-provided application settings storage so that your preferences can be retained between uses.

5. Sharing and Selling Personal Data

The developer does not sell your personal information and does not share health, activity, or device data with third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

The Watch Face is not specifically directed at children and does not intentionally request names, email addresses, postal addresses, or other directly identifying personal information.

7. Data Security

The Watch Face is designed to minimize transmission of watch-face data to external servers controlled by the developer. Data security practices for Garmin and KPay services are governed by their respective policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if future versions add features requiring additional data access. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Halloween Animated Watch Face, please contact the developer through the developer contact information shown on the Garmin Connect IQ Store listing.
